# ADR-010: The job pipeline reads company job boards, applies on company sites only, and keeps the human's approval per batch

Status: accepted (2026-11-09).

Ulugbek wants a list of every job he can legally take (F-1 student, STEM degree, no EU passport: USA, Europe, UAE), with employers that sponsor visas first, about 20 applications a day, a cover letter per job in his own voice, and a dashboard. Decisions:

- **Where jobs come from:** the public JSON of company job boards (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workable, SmartRecruiters), one request per company per scan, no login. Not LinkedIn, Handshake or Indeed: their terms forbid automated applying and accounts get flagged; they can still be used by hand to find companies, which are then added by their careers link. Workday needs an account per company and is left to him. Endpoint list from career-ops (MIT).
- **Who can sponsor:** the governments' own lists, downloaded on request (about 13 MB): the UK Home Office register of licensed sponsors (Skilled Worker route) and the USCIS H-1B Employer Data Hub (approvals per employer; the newest yearly file is FY2023). Matching is by normalised company name with a few allowed suffixes; a job also counts when its posting offers sponsorship or relocation. The UAE needs no list: the employer always arranges the visa. The Dutch IND register has no download and is not used.
- **The rules are code** (`src/jobs/eligibility.ts`), not a prompt: location, target roles, level, years of experience, "no sponsorship", citizens-only and clearance sentences, then sponsorship per country. Filtering 2,000 postings costs nothing.
- **Approval per batch, not per submit:** the agent asks before every submit; for a batch he reviewed on the Jobs page, each job's own site is pre-approved with "submit without asking" (payments, deleting and posting still ask). One application per company per 30 days; the daily cap defaults to 20; a monthly API cap (default $25) stops the batch. Runs use the fast model; the cover letter uses the smart model.
- **No pretending:** a CAPTCHA or "verify you are human" is refused in code (`CAPTCHA_RE`), sign-ins go to him, a form that asks whether AI was used is answered truthfully, applications go out at a human pace (2 to 4 minutes apart, one at a time). A question nobody answers for 8 minutes sets the job aside as "needs you" and the batch moves on.
- **The voice is checked in code:** `voiceProblems` rejects template phrases, dashes, exclamation marks and the wrong length; a draft that fails is rewritten once. The PDF is written by hand (`src/jobs/pdf.ts`: one Helvetica font, Letter page), so no library and no browser tab is needed.
- **Storage:** `jobs` (every posting with its verdict and status), `jobCompanies`, `sponsorLists`, `jobSettings`; all writes under the storage lock. The job list is the spreadsheet (CSV export) and the dashboard reads it.

Not done: Workday and other login-only systems; discovering companies beyond the sponsor lists; learning from rejections.
